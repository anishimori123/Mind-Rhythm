# Mind Rhythm Sampler
### For the producer who has a rhythm in mind but can't play keys

> Voice-first today, mind-first tomorrow. Hum, beatbox, imagine -> output as you want.

This repo is the invention blueprint for an AI algorithm sampler / music production station.

**Core idea:** You play music with your voice or the rhythm in your mind, the app outputs it as production-ready drums, bass, melody, styled as you want.

## Why Now?
- Dubler 2 proved producers pay + for voice->MIDI
